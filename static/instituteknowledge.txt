It is a practical, hands-on accounting course rather than a general finance theory course, focused specifically on how to run day-to-day bookkeeping inside Tally.

The full syllabus for the Tally ERP 9 — Financial Accounting course at Smartech Training Institute starts with an introduction to accounting and company creation in Tally, then covers preparing a trial balance and handling order processing and voucher entries. Students learn godown (warehouse) entry, receipt notes, delivery notes, and rejection in and out notes, followed by manufacturing journal entries, inventory notes, and stock journal entries. The course also covers price lists, discounts, batch-wise details, interest calculation, tax ledger setup, and finishes with cheque printing, giving students a complete, practical walkthrough of day-to-day accounting inside Tally ERP 9.

The Advance Tally Prime & GST course at Smartech Training Institute is a career-based course that includes a certificate and builds on basic Tally skills to cover Tally Prime software along with full GST (Goods and Services Tax) compliance. This course suits students and working professionals who want advanced, GST-compliant accounting skills using Tally Prime, and it is a natural next step after the Tally ERP 9 — Financial Accounting course at Smartech. It is aimed at students who already understand basic bookkeeping and now need to handle more advanced business scenarios such as payroll, multi-currency transactions, and GST tax filing inside Tally Prime.

The full syllabus for the Advance Tally Prime & GST course at Smartech Training Institute covers ledgers, stock entry, voucher entry, debit-credit notes, stock and physical journals, price lists, batch-wise details, actual and bill quantity tracking, and MRS. Students also learn multi-currency accounting, cost centers, point of sale (POS) features, scenario management, zero value entries, payroll processing, and budgeting, plus generating reports. The course covers excise for dealers, tax invoices, F11 features, F12 configuration, and exporting data to Excel, and finishes with full GST coverage including SGST, CGST, IGST, and UTGST, along with practice and assignments to reinforce every topic.
